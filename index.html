Fintech & Wealth Management QA Guide

A complete manual testing guide written in basic English with real-world examples.

1. Stock Market & Investment Basics

Stocks / Equities
A stock is a small piece of ownership in a company.
Example: You buy 10 stocks of Tata Motors. You now own a tiny part of Tata Motors.
NSE & BSE Basics
These are the digital marketplaces where you buy and sell stocks in India. NSE = National Stock Exchange. BSE = Bombay Stock Exchange.
Example: You go to a fruit market to buy apples. You go to NSE to buy stocks.
Portfolio
A collection of all the different investments a person owns.
Example: My portfolio has 50% stocks, 30% Mutual Funds, and 20% Gold.
Holdings vs Positions
Holdings are stocks you bought previously and keep in your account. Positions are live trades you are doing right now today.
Example: You bought Reliance last year (Holding). You bought Infosys this morning to sell by evening (Position).
Buy / Sell Orders
A request sent to the broker to buy or sell a stock at a specific price.
Example: "Buy 5 shares of SBI bank at ₹600 each."
Mutual Funds Basics
A pool of money collected from many people. An expert manager uses this money to buy a mix of good stocks.
Example: Instead of buying 10 different stocks yourself, you put ₹1000 in a Mutual Fund, and the expert does the hard work for you.
Profit & Loss (P&L)
How much money you made (Profit) or lost (Loss) from your investments.
Example: Bought for ₹100, sold for ₹150. Profit = ₹50.
CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate)
The average percentage your money grows every year over a long time.
Example: If ₹10,000 becomes ₹20,000 in 5 years, the CAGR shows the smooth yearly growth rate (about 14.8%).
XIRR (Extended Internal Rate of Return)
A complex formula used to calculate profit when you deposit or withdraw money on different dates during the year.
Example: You add ₹100 in Jan, ₹50 in March, and remove ₹20 in July. XIRR calculates your exact true profit percentage.
Portfolio Rebalancing
Fixing your portfolio back to its original plan by selling what went too high and buying what went low.
Example: Your plan was 50% Gold, 50% Stocks. Stocks grew too much and are now 70%. You sell some stocks to go back to 50/50.
Nifty 50 / Sensex Basics
A list of the biggest top companies. It tells us the general health of the share market.
Example: If Nifty 50 is going up, it means the top 50 companies are doing well, and the market is happy.
Asset Allocation & Risk Profile
Asset Allocation is dividing money into different buckets (Stocks, FD, Gold). Risk Profile is testing how much risk a user can handle without panicking.
Example: A young user has a "High Risk" profile, so their allocation is 90% Stocks. An old user is "Low Risk", so allocation is 90% Fixed Deposits.
Broker Integration & Demat Account
A Demat account holds your digital shares safely. Broker integration is how an app connects to platforms like Zerodha or Groww to buy shares.
Example: You use a Wealth App to decide what to buy, but the app connects to your Zerodha Demat account to actually do the buying.

2. Wealth Management Domain Knowledge

RIA & RA
RIA: Registered Investment Adviser (gives advice on whole portfolio).
RA: Research Analyst (researches and says which stock is good/bad). Both need government (SEBI) licenses.
Example: An RA says "Buy Tata Motors". An RIA says "You should invest 20% of your salary in these 5 stocks".
Client Onboarding Flow & KYC Process
The steps to register a new user. KYC (Know Your Customer) means proving who they are using ID cards.
Example: The user enters name, uploads PAN card photo, does a live video check, and signs a digital agreement.
Advisory Subscription & Fee Plans
How the advisor gets paid. Clients buy plans (monthly/yearly) to get advice.
Example: A client pays ₹500/month flat fee, or pays 1% of the profit they make, using auto-pay from their bank.
Investor Profile Management
Storing details about the user's age, salary, goals, and risk level.
Example: Profile shows user wants to save for a "House in 5 years" and has "Medium Risk".
Portfolio Recommendation Flow
The steps an advisor takes to send an investment plan to a client, and the client approving it.
Example: Advisor clicks "Send Suggestion". Client gets email, opens app, clicks "Accept and Invest".
Client Communication Workflow
How advisors talk to clients securely inside the app keeping an official record.
Example: Sending market updates, chat messages, or PDF reports directly inside the app instead of WhatsApp.

3. Trading Workflow Testing

Login → Portfolio → Buy/Sell → Order Placement
The happy path of a user coming into the app and completing a trade.
Example QA: Log in, check balance, search "Wipro", enter 10 qty, click Buy. Check if it goes to exchange.
Order Status: Confirmation, Failed, Pending, Cancelled
Testing all states a trade can be in.
Example QA: Buy after market hours -> Status should be "Pending". Try to buy without money -> Status should be "Failed". User cancels order -> Status "Cancelled".
Portfolio Update After Trades
Making sure numbers change correctly immediately after a trade happens.
Example QA: You had 5 shares. You bought 2. The Portfolio screen must instantly show 7 shares without logging out and back in.
Advisor vs Client Permissions & Roles
Checking that different users only see what they are allowed to see.
Example QA: Log in as Client -> "Delete Account" button is hidden. Log in as Advisor -> Can see all clients.

4. Financial Calculations Validation

Percentage, P&L, CAGR, XIRR Validation
Testing if the math formulas used by developers are completely accurate.
Example QA: Take the data shown on the website, put it in Microsoft Excel, use the `=XIRR()` formula. Both answers must match exactly.
Portfolio Allocation %
Checking if the pie chart slices add up correctly.
Example QA: 40% Stocks + 60% Gold = 100%. If system allows 101%, that is a bug.
Brokerage Basics & Tax/GST Basics
Testing the extra fees taken during trades. Brokerage is the platform fee. GST is government tax.
Example QA: If you buy ₹100 stock, total cost might be ₹100.50. You must test if the extra 50 paisa was calculated correctly using GST logic.
Decimal Precision Checks & Rounding Validation
Finance deals with exact paise/cents. Systems must not round numbers incorrectly.
Example QA: If profit is ₹10.556, it should round to ₹10.56. If it rounds to ₹11, it will cause huge accounting problems. Always test with 4 decimal places.

5. API Testing Basics

REST APIs & JSON Basics
API is how the frontend app talks to the backend database. JSON is the simple text language they use to send data.
Example: App asks API "Give me Apple price". API replies in JSON: {"stock": "Apple", "price": 150}.
Request/Response Validation
Checking if what you send (Request) gets the correct answer (Response).
Example QA: Send a request to buy -10 stocks. The response should be an error message, not a success message.
Status Codes & Authentication Tokens
Status codes tell you if it worked (200 OK) or failed (404 Not Found, 500 Error). Tokens are digital keys to prove who you are.
Example QA: Try to use the API without sending your login Token. Status code should be 401 (Unauthorized).
Tools: Postman & Swagger
Software used by testers to test APIs without needing a website screen.
Example QA: Open Postman, paste the API link, click Send, and look at the JSON result directly.

6. Database Testing Basics

SQL Basics (SELECT, WHERE, JOIN, Aggregate)
The language to ask questions to the database. SELECT gets data, WHERE filters it, JOIN connects tables, Aggregate does math (SUM).
Example: SELECT SUM(amount) FROM trades WHERE user_id = 5; (Checks total trade value for user 5).
Data Validation, Transaction Verification & Portfolio Checks
Checking if the data stored in the backend matches what happened on the frontend.
Example QA: You buy a stock on the website. You open the Database and check if the stock quantity increased by exact amount in the "portfolio_table".

7. Manual Testing Concepts

Test Cases & Scenarios
A scenario is "What to test" (Test Login). A test case is "How to test" (Enter email, password, click button, expect success).
Example: Scenario: Test Buying. Case 1: Buy with money. Case 2: Buy without money.
Types of Testing
  • Smoke: Basic check if app starts (Is login working?).
  • Regression: Checking if new changes broke old working code.
  • Sanity: Quick check of a specific new feature.
  • Functional/UI/Integration: Checking logic, checking buttons look good, checking modules connect.
  • Exploratory: Testing randomly like a real user without a written plan.
Defect Lifecycle & Bug Reporting
The journey of a bug: New -> Assigned -> Fixed -> Retest -> Closed. A bug report explains how the developer can see the bug.
Example Bug Report: "Steps: Go to Cart. Click Buy. Expected: Success. Actual: System Crash Error 500."
RTM Basics & Tools (Jira, TestRail)
RTM (Requirements Traceability Matrix) links requirements to test cases so nothing is missed. Jira is software to track bugs. TestRail stores test cases.
Example: Requirement "Allow UPI Payment" is linked to Test Case #45 in TestRail.

8. Security Testing Basics

Authentication & Authorization (Role-Based Access)
AuthN is "Are you real user?" (Login). AuthZ is "What are you allowed to do?" (Permissions).
Example QA: Try to access the URL `/admin-panel` while logged in as a normal user. It should block you.
Session Timeout & Password Rules
App should log out automatically if idle to protect data. Passwords must be hard to guess.
Example QA: Leave the screen idle for 15 minutes. Try to click a button. It should ask you to log in again.
OTP Validation & Data Masking
OTP ensures phone ownership. Data masking hides secret info like bank accounts.
Example QA: Test wrong OTP 3 times. It should lock. Check if Account Number shows as `XXXX-XXXX-1234` instead of full number.

9. Payment & Subscription Testing

Purchase Flow & Success/Failure
Testing the journey of paying money through a gateway.
Example QA: Turn off internet during Bank OTP page. App should show "Payment Failed" and NOT give the subscription.
Renewal Workflows & Failed Payments
Testing what happens next month when auto-pay happens.
Example QA: If user's credit card expired, the system should send an email "Action Required" and stop their advisory services.
Invoice Generation & GST Calculations
System must create a PDF receipt with correct tax added.
Example QA: Plan is ₹100. GST is 18%. Total charge must be ₹118. The PDF Invoice must show these split numbers clearly.

10. Reports & Dashboard Testing

Financial Reports Validation & Export
Testing tables of data and making sure the "Download PDF/Excel" button works.
Example QA: Download the Excel file. Check if the columns match the website. Check if numbers changed formatting (e.g. text instead of numbers).
Charts/Graphs Validation
Visual testing if the pie chart or line graph matches the actual data.
Example QA: If user has 0% Gold, the Pie chart should not have a Gold slice.
Filters, Sorting, Pagination & Search
Testing UI tools used to find data in long lists.
Example QA: Search for "Apple". List shows Apple. Click Sort by Date. List rearranges. Go to Page 2. Ensure data loads correctly.

11. Important Fintech Edge Cases

Decimal, Currency & Large Values
Testing extreme money numbers and formats.
Example QA: Input ₹99,999,999.99. Does the UI break? Does it show commas correctly (₹9,99,99,999)?
Negative Balance & Duplicate Transactions
Testing bad states. Users shouldn't have less than 0 money. They shouldn't be charged twice.
Example QA: Double-click the "Pay Now" button really fast. Does it charge you twice? (It should disable button immediately).
Date/Timezone & Market Timing Issues
Testing time limits. Markets close at specific times. Timezones differ.
Example QA: App is used in USA but trades in India. Does it use IST (Indian Time) correctly to block trades at 4:00 PM IST?
Incorrect Portfolio Totals
Testing if individual rows equal the grand total at the bottom.
Example QA: Stock A = ₹50, Stock B = ₹50. Grand Total must say ₹100. Always check totals manually.

12. Most Important Skills to Focus On (Priority Order)

  • Finance domain basics: Understand stocks, portfolios, and how the market works.
  • Trading workflow understanding: Know exactly what happens from clicking "Buy" to order confirmation.
  • Financial calculations validation: Math must be perfect. Learn to check XIRR and percentages in Excel.
  • SQL basics: Learn to look inside the database to verify data.
  • API basics with Postman: Be able to test backend logic without a frontend website.
  • Manual testing fundamentals: Know how to write good test cases and bug reports.
  • Security & role testing: Make sure clients can't act like admins.
  • Reporting/dashboard validation: Ensure data exports and charts are accurate.
